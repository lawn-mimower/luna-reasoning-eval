## Indian numerals and currency
- 1 lakh = 100,000 = 10^5. 1 crore = 100 lakh = 10,000,000 = 10^7.
- Convert to a common unit before comparing or adding quantities given in different units.
- Rs, Rs., and "rupees" all mean Indian Rupees. Never convert to another currency.
- Numbers written as words are operands: "three crore rupees" = 30,000,000; "twenty one days" = 21.
- Indian digit grouping reads as ##,##,##,### (8,20,000 = 820,000), not thousands-grouped.

## Comparison operators
- "at least" / "not less than" / "minimum of" mean >= .
- "more than" / "exceeds" / "in excess of" mean > (strict).
- "up to" / "not exceeding" / "not more than" / "shall not exceed" mean <= .
- "whichever is lower" means min(...); "whichever is higher" means max(...). Compute both
  arms before selecting. Never average them, never default to the first-listed arm.

## Indian financial year
- The financial year runs 1 April to 31 March.
- "FY 2024-25" means 1 April 2024 to 31 March 2025.
- "the previous five years" from a filing means the five completed financial years.
- "as on 31 March of the financial year" means the closing balance-sheet date.

## Dates
- "N days from date D" means D + N calendar days, counting the day after D as day 1.
- "a period of N days from the day of X" counts the day of X as day 1, ending at X + N - 1.
- "N months/years from D" means the same calendar date N months/years later, not a day count.
- N weeks = N x 7 days.
- "at least N days prior to event E" means the deadline is E - N days.
- "calendar days" includes weekends and holidays; "working days" excludes them.
- Where two trigger dates compete ("whichever is later"), take the later, then apply the period.

## Regulatory reading
- Where two passages conflict and one carries a later effective date or is marked an
  amendment or substitution, the later text governs.
- A general rule plus a narrower carve-out ("Provided that...", "Notwithstanding...") is a
  rule-and-exception pair, not a contradiction.
- Provisions imposing the same limit on different subjects, or addressing different stages,
  are consistent with each other.
- A repeal or savings clause is prospective only; actions already taken remain valid.
- "the Board" in a SEBI regulation means SEBI. "the Reserve Bank" means the RBI.
- Use only the definitions given in the passage. Do not substitute an outside definition.
- If a cross-reference resolves to a value not present in the passage, name the
  cross-reference rather than guessing the value.
